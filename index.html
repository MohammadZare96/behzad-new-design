About

Behzad Ahi was born in Tehran, Iran, in 1991. Since July 2023 he has been an assistant professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology.

Prior to joining the faculty, he was a postdoctoral researcher granted by the Iran National Science Foundation (INSF) from July 2020 to March 2022. He is currently director of the Data Fusion and Control Systems Lab.
